# Support

Use the channel that matches the request:

- **Setup and usage:** consult the [quick start](QUICK_START_GUIDE_NASH.md) and [documentation map](docs/README.md) first.
- **Reproducible product defect:** open a bug report using the repository template.
- **Feature or documentation proposal:** open a feature request or discussion, when enabled.
- **Security vulnerability or exposed secret:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report it privately.
- **Commercial, legal or licensing question:** contact Isaza Labs LLC through the contact route published by the repository owner. Do not use a public issue for confidential terms.

No public support service-level agreement is in force unless it is established in a separate written agreement.
