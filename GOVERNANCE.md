# Project governance

## Stewardship

Isaza Labs LLC is the owner, project steward and licensor of Nashira. NetworkingDev transferred the applicable Nashira rights to Isaza Labs LLC. Stewardship includes release authority, repository administration, security coordination, licensing decisions and protection of the Nashira identity.

## Decision model

- Maintainers can accept, request changes to or reject contributions.
- Material architecture, compatibility, security and licensing decisions are recorded as decision records.
- A maintainer with release authority approves published releases and their license metadata.
- Security matters are handled privately until coordinated disclosure is appropriate.
- Contribution does not confer ownership, governance authority or permission to use Nashira trademarks beyond the rights granted by the applicable license.

## Contributions

Code, workflows, skills, specifications and documentation are accepted at the maintainers' discretion under the Apache License 2.0 contribution model and the DCO process described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Changes to governance

Isaza Labs LLC can update this governance model as the maintainer group and release process mature. Material changes should be documented with an effective date and must not retroactively alter rights already granted under a published software license.
