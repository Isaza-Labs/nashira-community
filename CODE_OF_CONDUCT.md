# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must remain professional, respectful and useful. Participants are expected to discuss ideas and evidence rather than people, accept constructive review, respect privacy and attribution, and avoid language or conduct that a reasonable person would consider intimidating, discriminatory, harassing or disruptive.

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, impersonation, plagiarism and retaliation against a good-faith reporter.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review channels and project events, and when someone represents the Nashira project or Isaza Labs LLC.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit or remove content, issue warnings, limit participation or ban a participant when necessary to protect the project and its community. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and free from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository owner or organization administrator through the owning GitHub organization's private contact route. Do not publish personal or sensitive allegations in an issue.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1. Its adoption does not incorporate names, contacts or enforcement commitments that have not been approved by Isaza Labs LLC.
